Does this browser keep its session key?

Part of experiment S4. Takes about a minute, and tells us whether Safari will hold on to the key that protects your session after you log in.

What this is testing. After sign-in our page makes a secret key that is never allowed to leave the browser. Every later request is signed with it. If the browser throws that key away, users have to sign in again more often. This page finds out whether it does.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Everything happens in this page, and no data leaves your machine.
1
Create a test key
Stores a key in this browser's storage for this page.
2
Quit the browser completely, then come back
Not just close the tab — actually quit the application. Then reopen this page. This is the part that matters: anything held only in memory will be gone.
3
Check whether it survived
Looks for the key, and confirms it is the same one rather than a new one.
